Brief notes on Artificial Intelligence:

Artificial intelligence (AI) is the ability of computers to perform tasks that usually require human intelligence. These tasks include learning, reasoning, problem-solving, and decision-making.

Types of AI

Machine learning:
A subset of AI that uses algorithms to analyze data and learn from it Deep learning:
A subset of machine learning that uses neural networks to perform predictive analytics
Generative AI:
A type of AI that can create new content like text, images, and videos based on user prompts
Narrow AI:
A type of AI that performs a specific task, like fraud detection or legal document processing
